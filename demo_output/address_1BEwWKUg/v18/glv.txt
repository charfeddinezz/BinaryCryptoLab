Interpretation: GLV splits one 256-bit scalar multiplication into two 128-bit ones, so it saves roughly a factor of 2. A discrete-log attack quoted at 2^128 becomes about 2^127 operations: the exponent does not change, and the barrier does not move.

Notes
  - The endomorphism is a property of secp256k1, not of this key: every valid public key satisfies all of these checks, so the result carries no information about who generated the key or how.
  - GLV accelerates a discrete-log search by roughly a factor of 2 in the constant, which leaves the 2^128 exponent unchanged. A speedup is not a break.
  - No private scalar is used, derived, or searched for: every value here is computed from public coordinates and public curve constants.
