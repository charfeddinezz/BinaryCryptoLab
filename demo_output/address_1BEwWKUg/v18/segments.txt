Notes
  - A non-zero fold is the normal outcome; its absence of information is the point of printing it rather than only printing the exception.
  - Adjacent XOR and adjacent differences are descriptive arithmetic, not tests: they have no null distribution and no p-value.
  - With 160-256 bits the balance chi-square can only detect a gross imbalance; a large p-value is not proof of randomness.
  - Autocorrelation at these lags is not independent of the bit-balance test; both read the same bits, so agreement between them is not extra evidence.
