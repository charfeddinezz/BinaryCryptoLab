Recommendations
  - No structural weakness was detected, but absence of evidence is not proof of entropy: only the generator's source of randomness can establish that.
  - Verify the key byte-for-byte against a second export before accepting a deposit.
  - Compare the mainnet P2PKH/P2WPKH addresses in the matrix with the address you expect; a mismatch means the key or the script type is wrong.
  - This tool does not compute private keys. The discrete logarithm problem on secp256k1 remains infeasible; treat any claim otherwise as fraud.

Limitations
  - P-values assume the null hypothesis that each coordinate is a uniformly random 256-bit integer; they say nothing definitive about the generator.
  - Fisher's method assumes the evidence families are independent. Correlated measurements inside a family are already collapsed, but the families are not perfectly independent, so the Bonferroni bound is reported next to it.
  - The randomness battery is designed for long streams; on 64 bytes its power is limited and it can only flag gross anomalies.
  - Structural analysis cannot confirm or refute that a key corresponds to a small scalar without solving the discrete logarithm.
  - No blockchain data is consulted: ownership, balance, and history are unknown.

Warnings:
  - The forecast is a statistical reading of a public key, not key recovery.
  - No blockchain data was queried; ownership, balance, and history are unknown.
  - Addresses for testnet, signet, and regtest are for testing only.
